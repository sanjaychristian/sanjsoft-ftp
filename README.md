# SanjSoft FTP

Downloads dated radio programs from FTP, FTPS and SFTP servers on a schedule, so a station's programs are always ready to air. Free for radio stations to use, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

- Downloads only files dated today or later, reading the date from the file name, and skips files you already have.
- Handles programs that play for several days or air on a different day than the date on the server.
- Writes Title, Artist and Album tags into MP3 and WAV files.
- Warns about files that are empty, too short or silent.
- Can add new files to iTunes or Apple Music.
- Imports your existing sites from FileZilla.

## Getting started

Unzip the file and open **START HERE.txt**. There is nothing to install. The app opens in your web browser, but runs entirely on your computer. **User Guide.txt** explains every setting.

**Windows:** the first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac:** drag **SanjSoft FTP** into your Applications folder and open it from there. The Mac version is signed and approved by Apple.
